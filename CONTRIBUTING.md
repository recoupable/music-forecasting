# Contributing

Open an issue for feedback on the [draft RFC](SPEC.md), questions about benchmark design, or proposals for data sources and baselines. Use a pull request for concrete changes to the specification.

When proposing a data source, describe its provenance, coverage, how to establish when observations became available, and what its terms permit researchers to share. Use synthetic examples when redistribution rights are unclear.

When proposing a benchmark, define the candidate population, forecast cutoff, outcome, evaluation window, missing-data policy, and comparison baselines. For experimental results, record dataset and strategy versions, the periods used for training and evaluation, and enough detail to reproduce the run.
